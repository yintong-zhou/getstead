# M3 Investments — implementation plan (decisions confirmed, under Codex review)

Spec (PROJECT.md): buys, sells, dividends and coupons; average cost; current value with manually entered prices; allocation; indicative capital gains and losses at 26%, or 12.5% for Italian government bonds. The app tracks, it never recommends. Base: `main` after `fix/m2-debt-nav1` and `feat/profile` are merged. Do not build before the plan is approved and the owner authorises the build.

Post-merge facts the build must respect: the product name is **getstead** (cookies `getstead_session` and `getstead_login_challenge`, Prisma global `getsteadPrisma`); DB objects keep the `stead_` prefix, so new tables are `stead_*`; mobile navigation labels must fit 48 px at 320 px without ellipsis (see `nav` in `components/dashboard-copy.ts`); the profile migration `20261122090000_profile` precedes the investments migration, so the new timestamp must sort after it.

## Decisions (owner confirmed all six on 2026-11-20)

1. **Quantities as exact decimals.** Units can be fractional (ETF savings plans, crypto to 8 decimals). Proposal: `Prisma.Decimal` (already shipped with Prisma, no new dependency), column `Decimal(28,10)`. Rejected: JS floats (forbidden by AGENTS.md) and integer micro-units (overflow for large crypto quantities). **Arithmetic:** Prisma's default `Decimal` keeps only 20 significant digits, so `server/investments/` uses its own `Decimal.clone({ precision: 60, rounding: Decimal.ROUND_HALF_UP })`. Inputs are parsed from strings only (never numbers), non-finite values are rejected, and scale above 10 (quantity) or 8 (price) is rejected with 422. Decimals are converted to integer cents before they meet the number-based net worth code, **inside a safe range**: every `*Cents` column has a CHECK of at most 1e12 cents (€10 bn); a holding's value (quantity × unitPrice × 100) must also be at most 1e12 cents, enforced by the service on every transaction, quote and restore write (422 `value_out_of_range`, no silent clamping); hard caps per user: at most 1,000 instruments and 10,000 investment transactions (voided included), and each transaction `*Cents` column at most 1e11 (tightened from 1e12 for this reason), so cash, income, cost basis and realised gain each accumulate to at most 10,000 × 1e11 = 1e15, and holdings to at most 1,000 × 1e12 = 1e15, all below `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` (about 9.007e15). Accumulation inside `server/investments/` runs in `bigint` (cents) or the Decimal clone, and one checked converter `toSafeCents()` is the only way to cross into numbers: it throws a typed error that the API maps to 422 `value_out_of_range` on writes, and the net worth combination (M2 totals + investment cash + holdings) is checked the same way before formatting. Tests: a single value over the cap (`9007199254740993` units at €0.01), 10,000 repeated dividends of 1e11 cents on one instrument (exact result, no overflow), the 10,001st transaction rejected, and a forced combined-total overflow returning the typed error instead of throwing in `formatEuros`. Tests include quantities beyond 20 significant digits (e.g. `10000000000.0000000001 + 0.0000000001`).
2. **Money as integer cents, unit prices as exact decimals.** Trade amounts, fees, dividends and values are integer cents. A unit price is **EUR per unit**, `Decimal(18,8)`, because some instruments cost less than one cent per unit. Value in cents = round-half-up(quantity × unitPrice × 100), done only in `server/`. Worked examples (tests): 2 units at €12.50 = 2,500 cents; 3 units at €0.00333333 = 1 cent (0.999999 cents rounds to 1).
3. **Cost method: weighted average cost** (costo medio ponderato), the usual Italian method per instrument. Rejected for v1: FIFO and LIFO, tax-lot tracking.
4. **EUR only in v1.** Foreign-currency instruments are entered at the EUR amounts the owner actually paid or received. No FX table.
5. **Net worth integration, no double counting; cash is derived.** For `BROKERAGE` and `CRYPTO` accounts, a balance check is the **cash** balance at the end of its day. Investment transactions move that cash by a derived rule, with the same cutoff as movements (only rows dated after the latest check and up to D count; rows dated on the check day are already in it): BUY `−(amount + fees)`; SELL `+(amount − fees − taxWithheld)`; DIVIDEND and COUPON `+(amount − taxWithheld)`. `balanceAt` in `server/net-worth/calc.ts` takes these effects as an extra signed series, so a €600 buy after a €1,000 cash check gives €400 cash + €600 holdings = €1,000. Net worth = M2 total (with those derived cash effects) + market value of holdings. **No linked movements are created**: investment trades never appear in the cash flow register, budget or monthly income/expense in v1 (stated in the UI and README), and the owner must not also enter a movement for the same trade (the UI says so next to the transaction form and the balance-check form). Cash moved into the broker stays a normal transfer movement. Voiding an investment transaction removes its cash effect.
6. **Prices.** One manual price per instrument and date. Market value at date D uses the latest price on or before D; with no price it falls back to the average cost and is flagged "no price".

## Model

- `Instrument`: `id`, `userId`, `name`, optional `symbol`/`isin`, `kind` (`STOCK | ETF | FUND | IT_GOV_BOND | BOND | CRYPTO | OTHER`), `archivedAt`. Unique `(userId, name)`. `IT_GOV_BOND` means an **Italian government bond** and is the only 12.5% class; foreign government bonds use `BOND` (26%). The tax class is derived from the current `kind` by a pure function, so changing `kind` changes every historical estimate (indicative figures, documented).
- `InvestmentTransaction`: `id`, `userId`, `accountId` (a brokerage or crypto account of the same user), `instrumentId`, `type` (`BUY | SELL | DIVIDEND | COUPON`), `tradeDate`, `ordinal` (integer, same-day execution order; assigned as max+1 for that user, instrument and date inside the write transaction, editable), `quantity` (BUY/SELL only, > 0), `amountCents` (gross, > 0), `feesCents` (>= 0; **must be 0 for DIVIDEND and COUPON**, CHECK constraint and 422, so income has no fee), `taxWithheldCents` (>= 0, DIVIDEND/COUPON/SELL), `voidedAt`. Foreign keys include `userId` like M1 and M2, `ON DELETE RESTRICT`. **Replay order** for every calculation and every negative-position check is `(tradeDate, ordinal, id)`, never query order.
- `PriceQuote`: `id`, `userId`, `instrumentId`, `asOf` date, `unitPrice` Decimal. Unique `(userId, instrumentId, asOf)`.
- All tables get CHECK constraints (ranges, sign rules) and a block in `db/grant-runtime.sql`. Every query is scoped by `userId`.

## Pure calculation (`server/investments/calc.ts`, node:test, synthetic data)

- `position(transactions, asOf)`: quantity held and average cost. A BUY adds `amountCents + feesCents` to total cost. A SELL removes `round(totalCost × soldQty / heldQty)` and realises `proceeds − fees − removedCost`. Selling more than the held quantity is rejected (422), also when a backdated edit, a void, a restore or an instrument reassignment would make any replayed history negative.
- `holdingsValue(positions, quotes, asOf)` and `allocation(holdings)`: share by value and by `kind`.
- `income(transactions, range)`: dividends and coupons, gross and net of withholding.
- `indicativeTax(realisedByYear, kinds)`: 26% or 12.5% of **positive** gains per instrument class. Losses are shown, never carried forward (out of scope). Every figure carries the label "indicative estimate, not a tax statement".
- Tests: one buy; several buys and a partial sell (rounding); sell all; sell too much; backdated sell; voided rows ignored; same-date BUY/SELL/BUY in different `ordinal` orders (and shuffled input) give the defined result; voiding a buy that supports a later sell is rejected; price fallback; 12.5% bond class; gains and losses netted per tax class.

## Service, API, UI

1. **Schema and migration** `db/migrations/<ts>_investments/migration.sql` plus grants. Apply to Neon only with explicit approval.
2. **Service** `server/investments/service.ts`: CRUD for instruments, transactions (void instead of delete, like movements), quotes; `getPortfolio(userId, now)` (holdings, allocation, realised gain by year, income, indicative tax) and a 12-month market value series. Validation goes through `Reader` in `server/cash-flow/validate.ts`. No amounts in logs.
   **Write serialization:** every create, edit, void and restore runs in one transaction that first locks, in this fixed order (no deadlocks), the account row and then the instrument row(s) with `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` (both the old and the new instrument when an edit reassigns), then replays the whole affected history and rejects on a negative position. Updating an account's type (`updateAccount` in `server/cash-flow/catalog.ts`) locks the same account row and returns 409 if it would leave `BROKERAGE`/`CRYPTO` while investment transactions exist. Tests: two concurrent sells of 6 from 10 units (one wins, one 422); concurrent type change vs. transaction create; existing accounts API rejects the incompatible change.
3. **Net worth hook**: `getNetWorth` loads investment transactions and quotes inside the same RepeatableRead transaction, and adds holdings value plus the derived cash effects (decision 5) to **both** calculations in `server/net-worth/calc.ts`: `netWorthAt`/`balanceAt` for current totals and the independent loop in `monthlySeries` for the 12-month history (same date, check-cutoff and void rules; `changeCents` in `service.ts` derives from that history, so it follows). Tests: extend the existing history-parity test (series vs. `netWorthAt` reference) with investment effects, and require the final series point to equal the current totals. The dashboard card follows automatically.
4. **API** `app/api/investments/**` via the existing `api()` wrapper: `instruments`, `transactions`, `quotes`, `portfolio`.
5. **UI** page `/[locale]/investments` (EN and IT): hero with total value and unrealised gain, holdings table (quantity, average cost, price, value, gain), allocation bar, add-transaction form, quick price entry per holding, income and realised-gain/tax cards with the estimate label. Nav item: the mobile bar becomes **7 columns** (`grid-template-columns` in `app/globals.css`, about 36 px per item at 320 px, so existing labels such as "Ricorrenti" no longer fit). Rule: labels stay visible from 360 px up; below 360 px the items show icons only, each with its accessible name (`aria-label`) and visible focus. Content clearance (`.page` bottom padding, now 112 px) is re-checked so the last focusable control is never under the bar. Check all 7 destinations in EN and IT at 320, 390 and 1280 px. Glass style and brand guidelines, works at 320 px.
6. **Tests**: calc unit tests; integration tests for user isolation, constraints, runtime-role grants, negative-position rejection, net worth including holdings and derived cash effects (buy after a balance check, check-day cutoff, void).
7. **Docs**: README section; handoff.
